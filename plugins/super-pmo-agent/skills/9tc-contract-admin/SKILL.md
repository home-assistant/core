# 9TC Contract Administration

## Configuration

Load `../../config/project.json` before preparing notices, claim reviews, variation registers, payment schedules, or contract-administration recommendations.

## Workflow

1. Identify the contract, claim, variation, notice, or instruction being administered.
2. Separate approved, pending, disputed, rejected, and provisional items.
3. Confirm source evidence before recommending payment, approval, withholding, or escalation.
4. Treat unsigned or verbal variations as unapproved unless the executed contract and current law clearly say otherwise.
5. Keep advice practical and provisional; flag when solicitor, building surveyor, independent inspector, quantity surveyor, or other professional review is required.

## Progress claim and payment schedule checks

Check and report:

- Whether the claim is in writing and references the relevant contract stage or claim basis.
- Whether the claimed stage is physically complete by site inspection, independent inspector report, or required certificate.
- Mandatory inspection status, building surveyor records, unresolved defects, disputed variations, DBI status, and previous-stage defects.
- Vendor, description, amount, GST, trade/package allocation, approval status, duplicate risk, attachment/source path, payment status, retention/security, and reconciliation to commitment and budget.
- Victorian SOPA response deadline where SOPA may apply. Treat 10 business days as the default prompt-to-check timeframe unless the contract/current law says otherwise.

## Variation checks

Track variation scope, cost impact, time impact, approval basis, contract entitlement, GST basis, source evidence, and budget/programme impact.

## Output

Use tables for registers, claim assessments, withholding reasons, and action lists. Include Status, Sources reviewed, Key findings, Actions / Owner / Due date, Assumptions and limitations, Next control point, and Checks.
